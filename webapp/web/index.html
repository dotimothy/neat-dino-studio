Settings

Models

One detector runs on the MLA at a time. A detector needs the text encoder too; downloads include it. Fast (576 px) suits the webcam; Accurate (768 px) finds smaller things. Tiny is the smaller Grounding DINO (Swin-T backbone, 172.2 M parameters): quicker, a little less accurate.

Reset MLA
Unloads the model and restarts the accelerator runtime. Use it when a load keeps failing.

Voice

Say what to find. Whisper turns your speech into text and a small LLM turns the request into phrases for the detector: “can you find the red cars and any dogs” → red car . dog . Both run on the MLA through LLiMa, next to the detector.

Voice Models

The microphone needs the page over HTTPS (this studio serves both on the same port). Hold the Speak button (or the space bar outside the text box) while talking.

About

Neat DINO Studio runs Grounding DINO (base) on the SiMa.ai Modalix MLA. The network is compiled into 30 MLA ELFs; between them the board's CPU does the deformable-attention sampling, the top-900 query selection and the box updates. In Live mode two frames are in flight: one on the MLA while the CPU samples the other.

Model: IDEA-Research/grounding-dino-base — Grounding DINO Base, 232.3 M parameters in total: Swin-B image backbone (86.9 M), BERT Base text encoder (108.9 M), 6-layer deformable encoder and decoder with 900 queries and the heads (36.5 M). Original weights, compiled to bf16. Fast runs it at 576×576 px, Accurate at 768×768 px (letterboxed).
